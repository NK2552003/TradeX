## Commercial accuracy

Publish the actual price, tax treatment, renewal cycle, cancellation method, plan limits and advertising treatment at purchase. Affiliate rates and payout rules come from the accepted programme schedule. Do not use one generic withholding rate for every partner or describe a local ledger state as external settlement. Any change to a commercial promise must be reflected in the relevant checkout, agreement and support guidance.

## Consent and installation

Optional analytics, advertising, personalisation and automatic attribution default off. The cookie choice is versioned and expires after 180 days. Browser installation is optional and independent of consent or payment. Suggestions follow browser eligibility and a seven-day dismissal cooldown. Unsupported browsers receive manual guidance; installation does not guarantee offline work or notifications.

## Support and privacy operations

Support uses the published contact. A ticket form sends only when a delivery service is connected; opening an email draft is not a sent message. Handle privacy and deletion requests with proportionate verification, a record of the requested scope and an explanation of lawful retention exceptions. Do not publish response deadlines, backup expiry periods or service guarantees that the actual operation cannot support.

## Publishing and change management

Keep all policy readers and exported documents aligned with the current authored source. Record version, date, material changes and the responsible release owner. Archive prior versions needed for acceptance or dispute evidence. A material new optional processing purpose needs an updated notice and a fresh choice where required. Product guides and technical references are distinguished by their content rather than repeated status labels in the library.

## Release verification

Test public help links, search, document navigation, mobile text scaling, keyboard access, consent persistence and withdrawal, automatic attribution, install event lifecycle and storage failures. For payments or ads, additionally verify real provider callbacks, entitlements, regional consent and network payloads in a controlled environment. Do not click live ads to test revenue. Review material legal and operational changes with the appropriate qualified advisers.
