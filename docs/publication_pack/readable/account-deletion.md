# Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains how to request account closure, review the scope of deletion and understand information that may remain for limited purposes.

## Requesting deletion

Open Privacy settings and follow the available account-deletion flow. Review the selected account, content categories and confirmation carefully before submitting. If the control is unavailable or you cannot access the account, email h82292245@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request. We may require proportionate identity verification to prevent unauthorised deletion. Effective date: 9 November 2026.

## Export before deletion

Use an available export function and inspect the downloaded result before confirming a destructive action. Request help for records not covered by an export. A request to export is not proof that an archive was generated, delivered or is complete. Preserve receipts and other records you need for your own obligations.

## Scope and request status

The request screen identifies available content categories and timing choices. A submitted request may require processing and verification; it is not an immediate confirmation that all records have been erased. Check the displayed status and any completion notice. A delayed or grace option applies only when actually offered, and restoration or cancellation is available only if the workflow supports it.

## Content and shared copies

Deletion can affect profile and workspace content, attachments, community material and other selected categories. Some messages or shared material may have copies held by recipients or independent external sites. We cannot recall screenshots or independent copies. Records needed to handle an abuse report or legal dispute may be retained for that limited purpose.

## Records that may remain

We may retain limited invoices, transaction or tax records, security events, fraud evidence and dispute information where necessary for an applicable legal obligation or legitimate operational protection. Retained information is restricted to its stated purpose rather than ordinary marketing use. Retention depends on the category, legal obligation and unresolved matter; we will explain relevant exceptions in the request response where permitted.

## Backups local storage and providers

Removal from active systems and expiry from backups can occur on different cycles. Service providers may also need to process relevant removal requests. Browser or device storage, including unsynchronised drafts, is separate from server records. Clearing it can erase local work and preferences. Third-party domain cookies may require browser or provider controls.

## Billing installation and consent

Cancel renewals through the relevant store or billing provider; deletion or uninstall does not itself cancel a subscription. Removing the browser app does not delete your server account. Rejecting cookies stops future optional processing under the connected controls and does not erase all historical information. For a separate privacy request, contact h82292245@gmail.com.

## Issues and follow-up

Keep the request reference and any completion message. If data you expected to be removed remains accessible, report the affected record and context without republishing it. We may need to distinguish a retained legal record, a recipient copy, cached display or a processing error. Mandatory privacy and consumer rights remain available.
