# Workspace User Guide and Frequently Asked Questions

Guide | Updated 2026-11-09

A practical starting point for using Alphajournal, finding controls and understanding the limits of the workspace.

## Start with your account

Create or sign in to your account, review your profile and choose the appropriate workspace. Use a unique password and keep verification messages private. Feature access depends on your account role, current plan and deployment. A visible preview does not prove that a connected service is live.

## Record and review trades

Open the journal area and record the instrument, direction, entry and exit information, size, fees and review notes supported by the form. Verify currencies, time zones and imported values before relying on summaries. Use consistent tags and strategies so later review is useful. A journal entry records analysis; it is not a broker order.

## Research notes and strategies

Use notes for research and strategies for a repeatable plan. Review visibility before publishing or sharing. Keep passwords, brokerage credentials and third-party confidential material out of notes and attachments. Confirm that saved changes appear in the intended account before closing a session.

## Charts and AI tools

Check source, time, units and market availability before interpreting charts. AI may produce incorrect facts, calculations and assumptions. Review output and verify it independently. Sending a prompt to an enabled external AI service is separate from storing a local journal entry; consult the provider disclosure before sending confidential data.

## Community and messages

Choose what to share, check audience settings and follow the Community Guidelines. Public posts can be copied by other people. Use available report or block actions for spam and abuse. Avoid sending money, credentials or identity documents in response to unsolicited investment messages.

## Settings and personalisation

Appearance changes theme and density where supported. Notification settings control product preferences; device permissions are separate. Privacy settings control available visibility, export and deletion options. Help and policies contains the complete document library, cookie preferences, installation guide and contact details.

## Free access and billing

Free access may be supported by Google AdSense after rollout. Review the actual checkout for limits, taxes, renewal and ad treatment. Do not infer payment success from a selected plan. For a missing entitlement, preserve the receipt and contact support; do not pay again simply because the interface has not refreshed.

## Why was my work not saved

Check connectivity and whether you are signed in to the expected account. Keep the unsaved text and retry the explicit save action if present. Do not clear site data or uninstall while unsynchronised work is important. Report the affected screen, time and any visible error without sending sensitive records.

## How do I export or delete data

Open Privacy settings and use the available export and deletion controls. Review the deletion scope and confirmation carefully. Download and inspect any export before destructive actions. Billing cancellation and account deletion are separate.
