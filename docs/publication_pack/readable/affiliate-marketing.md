# Affiliate Marketing and Brand Guidelines

Policy | Revision 2026.2-INR | Updated 2026-11-21

These guidelines (Revision 2026.2-INR) help affiliates describe AlphaJournal pricing, Monthly Month-1 referral offers, and commercial relationships accurately.

## Identify the relationship

Place a clear affiliate or sponsored disclosure near the recommendation and link. Tell the audience that you may receive commission for a qualifying referral. Do not rely on a distant profile statement or hidden hashtag to explain a material commercial connection. Effective date: 21 November 2026 (Revision 2026.2-INR).

## Use accurate pricing and offer claims

State current published INR plans and Month-1 referral offers accurately: Basic Monthly is ₹239 for Month 1 with a referral code then ₹299/mo from Month 2; Pro Monthly is ₹399 for Month 1 with a referral code then ₹499/mo from Month 2; Annual plans are ₹2,868/yr (Basic) and ₹4,788/yr (Pro) with no additional stacked coupon discount. Clearly state that referral offers are limited to one per new subscriber account. Never promise investment returns, guaranteed profits, or unregistered advisory tips.

## Use of brand assets

Use authorised AlphaJournal names and assets without implying that you are AlphaJournal or an official support channel. Do not create confusing domains, account names, app listings or modified logos. Stop using assets when authorisation ends. Request permission before making a material adaptation or a new use of our brand.

## Channels and recipient choices

Comply with the advertising, privacy and communication rules applicable to each channel and audience. Do not send unsolicited bulk messages, hide redirects or collect credentials. Respect unsubscribes and blocking. Use assigned referral links and authorised campaign parameters without overwriting another partner’s attribution.

## Financial and sponsored content

Do not turn a software referral into an unqualified investment recommendation. Do not display fabricated trading outcomes or imply that users will recover subscription costs through profits. Explain risks and disclose sponsorship. Google AdSense and affiliate promotion are separate programmes; neither implies Google endorsement of AlphaJournal.

## Monitoring and corrections

Keep the claims and offers in your active campaigns current with Revision 2026.2-INR. Correct inaccurate material promptly when notified. Report impersonation, copied promotions or suspicious attribution to h82292245@gmail.com with a link and description. We may require removal of misleading uses of the brand under the programme terms.
