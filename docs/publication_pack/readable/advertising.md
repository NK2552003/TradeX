# Advertising and Sponsored Content Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

How advertising may fund the free Alphajournal workspace, what users can control, and how commercial messages are distinguished from product content.

## Planned service

Google AdSense is the selected advertising provider for a future free-version rollout. No AdSense publisher tag or ad requests are enabled by this implementation. Inventory, regional eligibility and paid-plan treatment must be confirmed before activation. A cookie choice alone does not activate an advertising service.

## Where advertisements may appear

Use clearly labelled Advertisement or Sponsored placements in approved general-content areas. Do not place ads in sign-in, billing, deletion, consent, private messages, journal editors, private research, AI conversations, sensitive financial views or security workflows. Keep distance from navigation and destructive controls to prevent accidental clicks. Placement must not cover content, imitate alerts or interrupt saving work.

## Information and consent

Before enabling AdSense, disclose data sent to Google, all active storage, geographic processing and controls. Optional advertising defaults off. Personalisation additionally requires its own affirmative choice. Non-personalised ads can still need storage and consent. A certified Google CMP is a deployment dependency in the regions covered by Google requirements. The product preference screen does not replace that CMP.

## Protected workspace information

Never pass private account data, holdings, balances, journal or note text, uploaded documents, messages, AI prompts, credentials, tax identifiers or payout details as ad parameters. Do not place AdSense on private-content routes where page scanning could expose such information. Validate the actual network payloads, URLs and referrers, not only source-level configuration.

## Your controls

Open Help and policies, then Cookie preferences. You may reject all optional processing, permit advertising without personalisation, or change your choices later. Essential account use remains available. Changes apply on this device. Google provides additional controls at https://myadcenter.google.com/ and information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. These external controls supplement the app choices.

## Commercial independence

An ad is not a recommendation, verification of a broker or promise of returns. Advertisers control the destinations of their ads. Review their terms and privacy practices before sharing information or transacting. Paid partners and community sponsors must disclose their relationship near the relevant content.

## Free and paid plans

The free version may carry ads when enabled. The checkout must state the advertising treatment of every paid plan. We do not promise an ad-free paid experience until the plan and delivery system enforce it. Browser installation does not change your plan, ad eligibility or privacy choices.

## Invalid traffic and reports

Do not repeatedly click ads, use automation to inflate impressions, encourage clicks to support the app or reward ordinary ad interaction. Report deceptive or unsafe ads to h82292245@gmail.com with a screenshot, destination if safely available and approximate time. Do not click a suspicious ad to gather evidence. The team should review blocked categories and placements and escalate provider reports.

## Changes and rollout

Notify users before a material new ad purpose or provider becomes active and renew choices where required. Publish the provider inventory and approved effective date with the release. Keep records of ad-network and CMP configuration changes. For privacy concerns, email h82292245@gmail.com.
