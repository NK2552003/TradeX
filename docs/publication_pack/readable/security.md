# Security and Incident Communication Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains account-safety expectations, responsible reporting and how Alphajournal approaches security-related communication.

## Security responsibility

We use access controls and service safeguards appropriate to the functions provided and review reported weaknesses. No system is completely secure and no independent certification, uninterrupted service or guaranteed breach prevention is asserted by this policy. Effective date: 9 November 2026.

## Protect your account

Use a strong unique password, keep verification codes and session information private, and review the account you are using before sharing or exporting records. Use additional authentication controls where available. Sign out on shared devices. Do not store broker secrets, payment credentials or private keys in journals, public posts or support messages.

## Permissions and access

Only grant permissions needed for the feature you intend to use. Review external integration scopes and revoke access through supported controls when no longer needed. A browser installation is not a request for broad device access. Staff and service access should be limited to authorised operational purposes; contact us if you suspect inappropriate access.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email h82292245@gmail.com with the subject Security report. Include the affected feature, time, reproduction steps and minimal redacted evidence. Do not access another person’s records, disrupt availability, extract bulk data or publicly disclose exploit details while investigation is pending. This reporting route does not authorise testing against third-party services.

## Incident assessment and response

We assess reports, investigate relevant evidence and take steps appropriate to the incident, such as restricting access, correcting a defect, revoking credentials or restoring service. We may withhold details that would expose another user or compromise an investigation. Response and notification duties depend on the facts, contracts and applicable law.

## Communication with affected users

Where notice is required or appropriate, we will explain the known nature of the issue, affected information where determined, actions taken and steps users can take. We will not knowingly present an unverified incident as resolved. No fixed response or notification deadline is promised beyond applicable obligations. Use the contact above for follow-up and reference an existing case where available.

## Unsafe ads and impersonation

Report suspicious ads, fake support messages and copied sites with a redacted screenshot and destination if safely available. Do not click unsafe ads to investigate them. Alphajournal support does not need your password or one-time code. For unauthorised payments or immediate threats, contact the relevant financial institution or emergency service directly.
