# Acceptable Use Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

These rules apply to Alphajournal accounts, content, community interactions, integrations and affiliate activity. They protect users and the operation of the service.

## Lawful and authorised use

Use the service lawfully and only with information, accounts and systems you are authorised to access. Do not impersonate another person, misrepresent a regulated status or upload material that violates another person’s rights. Effective date: 9 November 2026.

## Security and service integrity

Do not steal credentials, probe other accounts, exploit vulnerabilities, bypass access controls, distribute malware or disrupt the service. Do not evade plan limits, automate abusive traffic or scrape protected content without permission. Report a suspected weakness privately with minimal evidence that avoids other users’ data.

## Content and conduct

Do not post unlawful, threatening, harassing, hateful, sexually exploitative, deceptive or rights-infringing material. Do not expose someone’s private financial or contact information without authority. Do not manipulate users through fabricated returns, guaranteed-profit promises, phishing or impersonation.

## Messages and promotion

Do not send spam, unsolicited bulk promotions or repeated unwanted messages. Clearly disclose commercial relationships and affiliate links. Respect recipient choices and community moderation. Do not use the service to coordinate market manipulation or promote unlawful financial activity.

## Subscriptions referrals and advertising

Do not falsify purchases, create fraudulent accounts, stuff referral identifiers, overwrite attribution or abuse chargebacks. Do not click your own ads, automate impressions, encourage artificial clicks to support the app or reward ordinary ad interaction. Advertising and affiliate revenue programmes have separate rules.

## Enforcement and reports

We may restrict or remove offending content, limit features, suspend an account or take other proportionate action for misuse, security risk or legal duty. We may preserve evidence where necessary.
