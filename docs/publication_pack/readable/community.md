# Community Guidelines and Content Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

These guidelines apply to posts, profiles, comments, messages, shared records and other community participation on Alphajournal.

## Respectful participation

Discuss ideas without threats, abuse, discriminatory attacks or targeted harassment. Do not expose private information or encourage others to harass a person. Users may disagree on markets and strategies; disagreement does not justify personal abuse. Effective date: 9 November 2026.

## Honest financial content

Do not fabricate trading results, hide material assumptions or present hypothetical records as verified live performance. Do not promise guaranteed returns, impersonate a regulated adviser or use the community to organise manipulation. Explain uncertainty and the basis of a claim. Community content is not personalised investment advice.

## Commercial disclosures

Disclose sponsorship, commissions, affiliate links and other material financial interests clearly near the relevant content. Do not disguise advertising as an independent recommendation or system alert. An ad or paid placement does not mean Alphajournal approves the promoted product.

## Rights and privacy

Share only material you own or have permission to publish. Remove account numbers, credentials and unnecessary third-party data from screenshots and attachments. Check visibility before posting. Public content may be copied by other people, and deletion cannot recall those copies.

## Spam and harmful activity

Do not post scams, phishing links, malware, unsolicited bulk messages, illegal content or repeated promotional material. Do not exploit children or share sexual abuse material. Use report and block controls where available; contact h82292245@gmail.com if the relevant tool is unavailable.

## Reporting and review

Provide the content link or identifier, approximate time and a concise description. Preserve a redacted screenshot if helpful without amplifying private or harmful content. Reports are reviewed in context and may result in removal, reduced access or account restrictions. A report is not a guarantee of immediate removal. We may preserve evidence for security or legal purposes.

## Appeals and repeat violations

If you believe moderation was mistaken, contact h82292245@gmail.com with the affected record, any case reference and your explanation. We may limit details where disclosure could expose another person or compromise an investigation. Repeated or serious abuse can lead to suspension or termination under the Terms, without limiting mandatory rights.
