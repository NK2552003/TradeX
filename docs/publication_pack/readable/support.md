# Support Complaints and Privacy Requests

Guide | Updated 2026-11-09

Use the following routes to report a problem, dispute a transaction, request privacy assistance or report harmful content.

## Current contact

Email h82292245@gmail.com for support, privacy, advertising and security concerns. Use a clear subject such as Account support, Billing review, Privacy request, Security report or Advertising report. This mailbox is the current contact supplied by the operator; no delivery or response-time guarantee is implied.

## Information to include

Include the account email if needed to locate your account, affected screen, date and time, device and browser version, reproduction steps and expected outcome. For billing include a transaction reference and a redacted receipt. Do not include passwords, session tokens, one-time codes, full card or bank numbers, or unnecessary trading records.

## Support form availability

The in-app ticket form sends only when a support service has been connected. A disabled form is not a submitted ticket. A failed request should retain your message for retry. Opening an email draft does not send it; review and send it in your email app. If no email app opens, copy the contact address.

## Privacy requests

Describe whether you need access, correction, export, erasure, consent withdrawal or help with visibility. We may ask for proportionate identity verification using a secure route. Never send identity documents unsolicited to a general mailbox. Request scope, applicable rights, exceptions and timelines must be assessed under the law that applies to you.

## Complaints and escalation

Keep your original correspondence and reference any case identifier in follow-ups. Explain why a previous response did not resolve the issue and the outcome you seek. The current contact above handles complaints and privacy requests. Applicable statutory complaint and response rights remain available. This process does not limit statutory consumer or regulatory remedies.

## Security and urgent issues

For suspected compromise, stop sharing credentials, change passwords through trusted controls and contact support. Report vulnerabilities privately with minimal proof that avoids other users data. This mailbox is not an emergency service. Contact relevant emergency or financial institutions directly for immediate threats or unauthorised payments.

## Handling and retention

Support information should be restricted to staff and processors resolving the case. Preserve only the evidence needed for resolution and applicable legal duties. Confirm retention periods and escalation ownership before publishing a fixed promise. Requests to remove a support record may be limited by an ongoing dispute or statutory retention requirement.
