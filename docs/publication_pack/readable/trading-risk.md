## Risk of loss

Trading and investing can lose some or all invested capital. Leveraged products, derivatives, short positions and certain arrangements can expose you to losses beyond an initial deposit or to rapid liquidation. Liquidity, volatility, execution, financing, counterparty and operational risks can change unexpectedly. Do not trade funds you cannot afford to lose.

## Historical results and simulations

Past performance, backtests, examples, hypothetical returns and journal statistics do not guarantee future results. Results depend on assumptions, fees, slippage, data quality, execution and selection bias. A profitable shared record may be incomplete, unverified or unrepresentative. Check methodology before comparing accounts or strategies.

## Market information and calculations

Prices and charts may be delayed, incomplete, incorrect or unavailable. Your entries, imports, time zones, instrument definitions, units, exchange rates and fees affect calculated results. Reconcile important figures against authoritative statements. No alert, chart or notification is a guaranteed execution, liquidation or risk-control mechanism.

## Artificial intelligence

AI can invent facts, misunderstand a prompt, make arithmetic errors and produce convincing but unsuitable output. Verify sources, numbers and assumptions independently. An AI label or confidence score is not regulatory approval or a probability of profit. Review external-provider disclosures before submitting confidential information.
