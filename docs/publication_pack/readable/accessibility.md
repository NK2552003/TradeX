# Accessibility and Device Permissions Guide

Guide | Updated 2026-11-09

Adjust the experience and understand what device access means before granting it.

## Reading and navigation

Use browser zoom, system text sizing, keyboard navigation and your screen reader as appropriate. The help library uses headings, labelled controls and expandable sections. If content is clipped or a control is difficult to reach, report the screen, device, text scale and assistive technology to h82292245@gmail.com. No independent accessibility certification is claimed.

## Theme and motion

Use the appearance controls offered by the app and system accessibility preferences. Contrast, focus and motion should be checked in both light and dark themes at release. Installation prompts and cookie choices should remain readable on small screens and at larger text sizes.

## Microphone and speech

A supported dictation or voice feature may request microphone access when you invoke it. Review the permission prompt and any speech-processing disclosure. Declining should leave typed input available where the feature supports it. Revoke microphone access in browser or OS settings when no longer needed.

## Files and attachments

A file picker gives access to the selected file or files; review what you select before upload. Attachments may contain names, account numbers or hidden metadata. Remove sensitive information before sharing and check the destination visibility. Do not grant broad access merely to read a policy or install the browser app.

## Notifications

Notification preferences in the app do not override browser or OS permission. Grant permission only when you want supported notifications. Delivery can be affected by connectivity, battery settings, permissions and platform restrictions. Do not depend on notifications as a guaranteed trading or risk alert service.

## Location camera and advertising identifiers

No blanket location or camera permission is required to read help or use cookie choices. Any future feature using additional access needs a clear purpose at the point of request. A browser consent screen is not iOS App Tracking Transparency permission and must not be presented as granting it.

## Revocation and shared devices

Use browser site settings or OS application settings to review permissions. Sign out on shared devices and avoid saving credentials. Clearing storage may erase unsynchronised work and consent preferences. Revoking a device permission does not delete server-side content or cancel billing.
