Policy | Revision 2026.2-INR | Updated 2026-11-21

These terms (Revision 2026.2-INR) apply to approved AlphaJournal partners and govern tiered per-user commissions, subscriber Monthly Month-1 referral discounts, the 50-paid-user Pro milestone, and strict affiliate coupon prohibitions.

## Joining the programme and no automatic free tier

An application does not itself create an approved partnership, and approval does not grant complimentary Basic or Pro access. Affiliates start on the Free tier (or may purchase Basic or Pro at standard pricing without using any referral/coupon code). Complimentary AlphaJournal Pro unlocks automatically only after 50 distinct referred users complete verified Basic or Pro payments, and is automatically revoked if refunds, chargebacks, or policy reversals drop verified paid referrals below 50 or if the partner is suspended. Effective date: 21 November 2026 (Revision 2026.2-INR).

You act independently and are not an employee, agent, investment adviser or authorised representative of AlphaJournal. You cannot bind us, promise product capabilities or offer unapproved commercial terms. You are responsible for your promotional channels, required permissions and applicable obligations.

## Referral links, offer validity and single-use limit

Use your assigned code and authorised campaign links. A user may apply an active partner's referral code during signup or at first paid checkout. Each user account may redeem at most one referral offer in its lifetime, valid only on their first paid Basic or Pro subscription while the issuing partner is active and within any configured offer validity window. Plan switches, upgrades, downgrades, and renewals do not generate duplicate discounts or duplicate commissions. Do not bypass user consent through fingerprinting, hidden redirects or cookie stuffing.

## Qualifying commissions and subscriber monthly discounts

Under Revision 2026.2-INR, approved affiliates earn ₹10 INR per verified distinct paying user for their first 50 Basic or Pro conversions (users #1–50), and ₹30 INR per verified distinct paying user starting from their 51st Basic or Pro conversion onward (users #51+). For referred subscribers, the referral discount applies exclusively to Month 1 of Monthly plans: Basic Monthly is ₹239 for Month 1 (₹60 off ₹299) and renews at ₹299/mo from Month 2; Pro Monthly is ₹399 for Month 1 (₹100 off ₹499) and renews at ₹499/mo from Month 2. Annual plans already include the 20% discounted rate (₹239/mo Basic = ₹2,868/yr; ₹399/mo Pro = ₹4,788/yr) and attribute the partner without stacking additional discount.

## Honest promotion

Clearly disclose that a qualifying referral may earn you commission in line with SEBI and ASCI guidelines. Use current approved product claims and brand assets. Do not promise investment returns, misrepresent regulatory approval, or advertise annual discounts as stacked referral discounts.

## Strict prohibition on affiliate coupon use and misconduct

Approved, active, pending, and suspended affiliate accounts are strictly prohibited from redeeming their own referral code, any other affiliate's referral code, or promotional referral coupons. Self-referral, circular referrals between affiliates, false accounts, payment fraud, forced redirects, attribution manipulation, spam, brand-keyword ads, and misleading coupon pages are strictly prohibited and result in immediate commission forfeiture and suspension.

## Adjustments, clawbacks and payout

Refunds, chargebacks, duplicate transactions, invalid referrals and disqualifying events reverse the associated commission and decrement the verified paid referral count (which may also revoke complimentary Pro if verified conversions fall below 50). Minimum INR withdrawal threshold is ₹1,000 via NPCI UPI, Bank NEFT, IMPS, or RTGS subject to PAN/KYC checks and 5% statutory TDS u/s 194H where applicable. See the Commission Payout and Tax Policy.
