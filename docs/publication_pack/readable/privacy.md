# Privacy Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains how Alphajournal handles information when you use our website, apps, trading journals, research tools, community and affiliate features. It also explains your choices and how to contact us about your data.

## Who we are and how to contact us

Alphajournal operates the service described in this policy. For support, privacy questions or a grievance, email h82292245@gmail.com with the subject Privacy request. Tell us the issue and the outcome you seek. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account before disclosing or changing personal information. This policy takes effect on 9 November 2026.

## Information you provide

We receive account and profile information such as email, handle, display name, authentication details and account role. When you use a feature, we may process the material you provide, including journal entries, notes, strategies, watchlists, messages, community posts, attachments and AI requests. Support correspondence may include a description of a problem and associated records. Provide only what is needed and remove third-party confidential information before uploading.

## Service billing and affiliate information

The service uses session, device, browser, network and security information to operate and protect accounts. Billing providers and app stores may provide transaction identifiers, purchase status, invoices and entitlements. Affiliate features can process applications, referral codes, campaign information, commission records and payment or tax details that you submit. Do not send card numbers, passwords or payment credentials to the support mailbox.

## How information is used

We use information to provide requested features, authenticate you, save and display your work, calculate workspace summaries, administer account access and plans, resolve support requests, prevent misuse and comply with applicable obligations. We process billing and affiliate records for the relevant transaction or programme. Optional measurement, advertising and automatic referral purposes are subject to the separate controls described below. We do not treat installation or continued browsing as agreement to optional tracking.

## Visibility and sharing

The audience for a record depends on the feature and visibility you choose. Review those settings before publishing or sharing. Public posts, links and shared material can be seen, copied or redistributed by recipients, and removing them from Alphajournal cannot recall independent copies. Private content is used to provide the features you request; it is not made public merely because you install the browser app.

## Service providers and external services

We use service providers for functions such as hosting, account authentication, data storage and payment administration. The code supports Supabase-backed account and data services and platform-specific billing integrations; actual provider use depends on the service and platform you access. If you invoke an external AI or integration feature, review its disclosure before submitting confidential information. Providers may process information in other countries under their applicable terms and legal safeguards. External websites have their own privacy practices.

## Cookies analytics and planned advertising

Essential storage supports sign-in, security and requested settings. Optional analytics, advertising, personalised ads and automatic affiliate attribution start off. Google AdSense is planned to support the free version; it is not active in this release. If enabled, ad requests can disclose device, browser, IP, page-context and identifier information to Google. Private journals, messages, notes, balances, credentials and tax or payment details are not advertising targeting inputs. Read the Cookie Policy and Advertising Policy for the scope of each choice.

## Keeping information

We keep account information and user content for the service while the account is active, subject to your deletion requests and the features available for removing individual records. We may retain limited information for legal, accounting, security, fraud prevention or unresolved disputes where necessary. Retained records are restricted to those purposes. Local cookie preferences expire after 180 days; automatic referral attribution is valid for less than 30 days. Backups and third-party records may have separate removal cycles, and account deletion is not a promise of instantaneous removal from every system.

## Your choices and requests

Use Privacy settings to review visibility and available export or deletion controls. Use Cookie preferences in Help and policies to withdraw optional choices on this device. For access, correction, erasure, export, information about processing or a grievance, email h82292245@gmail.com. Rights, verification, exceptions and response periods depend on the law that applies to your request. If information must be retained, we will explain the relevant reason where legally permitted. Withdrawal does not retrospectively invalidate earlier processing.

## Children and account safety

Alphajournal is intended for adults aged 18 or older who can enter a binding agreement. Do not create an account for a child or provide a child’s data without first contacting us. Use a unique password, keep verification codes private and sign out on shared devices. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected account compromise or unintended disclosure promptly through the contact above.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the service, providers or applicable requirements change. The current version and its date are available in Help and policies. We will provide notice of material changes and request a new choice where required before processing for a materially different optional purpose. Cookie consent, contract acceptance and device permissions remain separate decisions.
