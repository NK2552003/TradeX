# Affiliate Commission Payout and Tax Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains how to interpret affiliate balances, request payment and resolve payout or tax questions under your accepted programme schedule.

## Commission states

A tracked event may be pending, qualified, approved, reversed or paid. An estimate is not a settled entitlement. The accepted schedule determines the rate, eligible revenue, exclusions and qualification conditions. Refunds, chargebacks and invalid referrals can adjust an amount. Effective date: 9 November 2026.

## Statements and evidence

Review the relevant transaction or referral reference, gross commission, adjustments, deductions and net amount. Retain statements and payment confirmations for your records. An internal status label is not independent proof that a bank transfer settled. Report discrepancies with the affected references rather than sending full payment credentials.

## Payout eligibility

Payment may require approval, completed identity and tax information, a supported destination and the threshold or timing specified in your accepted schedule. Provide accurate information through the designated workflow. Do not submit another person’s bank or tax details without authority. Available payment rails can vary by region and account status.

## Tax treatment

You are responsible for your own tax reporting and professional advice. Alphajournal will apply deductions and issue or make available supporting records where required by the law applicable to the payment and recipient. No universal withholding rate is promised here; rates and treatment can depend on law, thresholds, residency and documentation. An in-app estimate is not a statutory tax certificate.

## Requests settlement and failures

Follow the payout request and review steps shown in the workspace. A request is not a guarantee of immediate transfer. Bank or provider processing, verification and operational reviews can affect completion. If a transfer fails, provide corrected details through the supported secure route and refer to the original request so the ledger can be reconciled.

## Disputes and fraud review

Email h82292245@gmail.com with the statement or payout reference and a concise explanation. We may hold an affected amount while investigating suspected fraud, duplicate credit, a refund or a legal obligation. Where permitted, we will explain the decision and correction. We do not silently treat every disputed amount as forfeited.

## Advertising revenue and termination

Google AdSense publisher earnings are separate from affiliate commission unless an accepted programme schedule expressly includes them. Do not combine estimated ad earnings with settled affiliate balances. On termination, qualified amounts and retained records remain subject to the accepted agreement, lawful adjustments and required tax or accounting duties.
