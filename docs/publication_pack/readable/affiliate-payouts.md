# Affiliate Commission Payout and Tax Policy

Policy | Revision 2026.2-INR | Updated 2026-11-21

This policy (Revision 2026.2-INR) explains the ₹10/₹30 tiered per-user INR commission ledger, 50-paid-user Pro milestone, payout requests, refund clawbacks, and Section 194H TDS.

## Commission schedule and states

Verified paid Basic or Pro conversions earn ₹10 INR per distinct user for conversions #1–50 and ₹30 INR per distinct user starting at conversion #51 onward. Each distinct referred user qualifies at most once; renewals, upgrades, and cycle switches do not create duplicate payouts. A tracked event may be pending, converted, reversed or paid. Refunds, chargebacks and invalid referrals automatically reverse commission and reduce verified conversion counts (which also re-evaluates the 50-user complimentary Pro milestone). Effective date: 21 November 2026 (Revision 2026.2-INR).

## Statements and evidence

Review the relevant transaction or referral reference, conversion slot (#1–50 vs #51+), gross INR commission, 5% u/s 194H TDS deduction, and net balance. Retain statements and payment confirmations for your records. An internal status label is not independent proof that a bank transfer settled. Report discrepancies with the affected references rather than sending full payment credentials.

## Payout eligibility

Payment requires an active approved affiliate profile, verified Indian PAN and UPI/Bank IFSC destination, and a minimum withdrawable balance of ₹1,000 INR. Do not submit another person’s bank or tax details without authority. Supported rails include NPCI UPI, Bank NEFT, IMPS, and RTGS.

## Tax treatment

You are responsible for your own tax reporting and professional advice. AlphaJournal applies 5% statutory TDS under Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, 1961 where applicable and supports verified GSTIN invoicing for GST-registered partners. An in-app estimate is not a statutory Form 16A certificate.

## Requests settlement and failures

Follow the payout request and review steps shown in the Partner Hub. Only one pending or processing payout may be open at a time. If a transfer fails, funds are restored to your unpaid INR balance so you can update destination details and retry.

## Disputes and fraud review

Email h82292245@gmail.com with the statement or payout reference and a concise explanation. We may hold an affected amount while investigating suspected self-referral, affiliate coupon misuse, duplicate credit, a refund or a legal obligation.

## Advertising revenue and termination

Google AdSense publisher earnings are separate from affiliate commission unless an accepted programme schedule expressly includes them. Do not combine estimated ad earnings with settled affiliate balances. On termination, qualified amounts and retained records remain subject to the accepted agreement, lawful adjustments and required tax or accounting duties.
