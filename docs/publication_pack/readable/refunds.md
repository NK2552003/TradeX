# Subscription Cancellation and Refund Policy

Policy | Updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains how to review purchases, stop renewal and request a billing or refund review. Your actual checkout and payment provider identify the offer you purchased.

## Purchase information

Before paying, review the plan, total price, currency, taxes, billing period, any trial and renewal conditions. Keep the receipt and transaction reference. Features depend on the entitlement confirmed by the billing provider. A selected plan, browser installation or incomplete checkout is not a confirmed purchase. Effective date: 9 November 2026.

## Renewals and trials

A subscription renews only according to the terms shown when you purchase it. If a trial or introductory offer is available, check when normal billing begins and the cancellation deadline shown by that provider. Do not assume every platform or plan offers the same trial. Review any price-change notice and the available cancellation options.

## How to cancel

For an Apple or Google store purchase, use that store’s subscription management controls for the purchasing account. For another billing provider, use the management or cancellation route shown on the receipt or in Billing settings. If you cannot locate it, email h82292245@gmail.com with the transaction reference. Uninstalling, logging out or submitting account deletion is not a substitute for cancelling renewal.

## What cancellation changes

Cancellation normally stops the next renewal under the provider’s terms; it does not automatically reverse past charges. Access may continue until the paid period ends unless a refund, chargeback or another entitlement change ends it earlier. Check the provider confirmation for the actual effective date. Download any needed records before losing access to a paid-only export feature.

## Requesting a refund review

For store purchases, use the store’s refund process where it controls the payment. For other purchases, contact h82292245@gmail.com with the account email, date, amount, transaction reference and reason. Do not include full card or bank details. We review the purchase conditions, the issue, provider rules and applicable consumer rights; this policy does not create an unconditional refund promise or remove a statutory entitlement.

## Duplicate charges and missing access

Report duplicate or incorrect charges and confirmed purchases that did not activate access. Keep the receipt and any error message. Check the purchasing account and use Restore purchases where offered before making another purchase. A support acknowledgement is not a refund settlement; the provider’s transaction status determines whether money was returned.

## Processing and disputes

Where a refund is approved, processing and the time funds reach you depend on the payment provider and financial institution. Currency conversion or third-party charges may be governed by their terms. If you dispute the outcome, reply with the prior case details and supporting evidence. You retain applicable statutory and payment-provider remedies.

## Advertising and account closure

A paid plan removes advertising only where its purchase terms say so. Withdrawing optional advertising consent does not cancel a subscription or change billing. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions, and legally required transaction records may remain after closure. Refer to the Terms and Data Retention Policy for related responsibilities.
