# TradeX Writing and Sharing

TradeX connects a trader's private working records with selectively published material while keeping ownership and visibility explicit.

## Language

**Writing record**:
A note, journal entry, or strategy created in the Writing Desk.
_Avoid_: Document, content item, asset

**Linked record**:
A Writing Desk relationship used to navigate between the thinking, execution, and playbook for a trade. It remains part of the private workspace unless each record is separately published.
_Avoid_: Post attachment, social link

**Post attachment**:
The optional single Writing record referenced by a social post. A post may reference one note, one journal entry, or one strategy—never several at once.
_Avoid_: Linked record, post media

**Public record**:
A Writing record its owner has explicitly made readable outside their private workspace. Attaching a record to a public post requires the record to be public.
_Avoid_: Shared record, unlocked record

**Private record**:
A Writing record readable only inside its owner's workspace. Its title, body, performance data, and linked records must not be exposed through a public post.
_Avoid_: Draft, hidden record
