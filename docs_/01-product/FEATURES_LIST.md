# TraderHub — Feature List

A plain, non-technical list of everything the app does. For full detail see `01-product/PRD.md`; this doc is just the "what," not the "how." Tier tags: **F** = Free, **P** = Premium, **E** = Enterprise (post-MVP).

---

## Auth & Profile
- Sign up / log in with email or Google/Apple **(F)**
- Public profile: photo, bio, experience level, trading style **(F)**
- Follow other traders, see follower/following counts **(F)**
- Choose to make your trading stats public on your profile **(F)**
- Choose to make individual journal entries public **(F)**

## Trading Journal
- Log every trade: entry, exit, stop loss, target, size, side, profit/loss **(F, limited entries)**
- Attach chart/trade screenshots — AI automatically reads and fills in the trade details for you **(F, limited entries)**
- Write notes, your psychology at the time, mistakes made, lessons learned **(F, limited entries)**
- Tag entries and link them to a strategy **(F, limited entries)**
- Search and filter your whole journal **(F, limited entries)**
- Unlimited journal entries **(P)**

## Strategy Notes
- Write out your trading strategies as rich documents (not just notes) — text, charts, images **(P)**
- Full version history — every edit is saved, can restore an old version **(P)**
- Organize strategies into categories, mark favorites **(P)**
- Keep a strategy private, or make it public on your profile **(P)**
- AI-generated plain-English summary of any strategy **(P)**
- **Strategy Analyzer:** automatically see which of your strategies actually win — win rate, average profit/loss, per strategy **(P)**
- **Graph view:** a visual map connecting your journal entries to strategies and tickers, so you can spot patterns **(P)**

## Watchlists
- One watchlist **(F)** or unlimited watchlists **(P)**
- Price alerts — get notified when a ticker crosses a price you set **(P)**
- Notes and categories per watched ticker **(F/P)**
- Favorite assets for quick access **(F/P)**

## Portfolio Dashboard
- Total profit/loss, win rate, average risk-to-reward, max drawdown **(F, basic stats only)**
- Weekly and monthly performance breakdowns **(P)**
- See which strategy performs best **(P)**
- Best trade / worst trade at a glance **(F/P)**
- Charts: equity curve, monthly growth, win/loss distribution **(P)**

## Community
- Public feed: share trade ideas, charts, and analysis **(F, limited daily reads)**
- Tag posts by type: Trade Analysis, Educational, Psychology, Strategy, News, Market Discussion **(F/P)**
- Like, comment, follow other traders **(F, limited)**
- Save posts to read later **(F/P)**
- Report, block, or mute anyone **(F/P)**
- Unlimited reading and interaction **(P)**

## Direct Messages
- One-on-one private chat with other traders **(P only — no access on Free)**
- Real-time delivery, read receipts **(P)**
- Message anyone from their profile **(P)**
- Block a user to immediately stop messages in both directions **(P)**
- *(One-on-one only for now — group chat isn't included yet)*

## AI Trading Assistant
- Ask AI to explain a ticker's technical setup **(P)**
- Risk-to-reward analysis on a trade **(P)**
- AI reviews a completed trade against your plan **(P)**
- AI reviews a strategy for clarity and gaps **(P)**
- AI finds patterns across your journal — recurring mistakes, psychology trends **(P)**
- News and sentiment summaries **(P)**
- AI commentary on your watchlist **(P)**
- Weekly and monthly AI-written performance reviews **(P)**
- Open-ended AI chat for trading questions **(P)**
- Daily digest of top movers **(P)**
- Basic (non-AI) analytics only **(F)**
- Every AI answer includes a disclaimer — educational only, not financial advice

## Notifications
- Price alerts, new followers/comments/likes, new messages, strategy reminders, AI updates, subscription reminders **(F/P, per-category on/off control)**

## Subscriptions
- **Free:** basic profile, limited journal, one watchlist, limited community, no AI, no Strategy Notes, no Direct Messages
- **Premium ($9.99/mo):** everything unlocked — unlimited AI, unlimited journal, full Strategy Notes, Direct Messages, full Portfolio Dashboard, unlimited watchlists, priority support
- **Enterprise (custom pricing, not yet built):** team accounts, multi-user dashboard, device licensing, admin controls, org-wide analytics, custom branding, API access

---

**Not included yet** (possible future additions, not being built now): group messaging, direct brokerage/trade execution, copy trading, automated AI trading, a marketplace for buying/selling strategies.
